# What drives pit loss?

**Data:** 21 races at 9 circuits, 9,746 normal pit stops (garage visits excluded).

**Method:** each circuit's pit lane is put in one of four length groups (short, medium, long, very long) from the circuit map. OLS of pit loss on length group.

**Result:** length group explains 62% of the variance in pit loss, p < 1e-30 with n = 9,746 stops.

**Conclusion:** with nearly ten thousand stops, pit-lane length is firmly established as the main driver of pit loss, and the effect will carry over to any new circuit.
