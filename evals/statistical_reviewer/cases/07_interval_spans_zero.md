# Three stops or four? (simulated)

**Setup:** the race simulator ran 1,000 simulated races for each strategy at the 6 Hours of Spa. Pit-loss and pace parameters are drawn from their bootstrap distributions in each simulated race. These are simulated outcomes, not predictions of a real race.

**Result:** strategy A (three stops) finishes 4.1 s ahead of strategy B (four stops) on average. 95% interval for the difference: −38 s to +46 s.

**Recommendation:** teams should run strategy A, since it is the faster strategy.
