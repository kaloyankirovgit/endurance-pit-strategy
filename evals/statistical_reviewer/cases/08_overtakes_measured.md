# Time lost per overtake

**Data:** 21 races, Hypercar laps only.

**Method:** using the sector timing, we counted 14,233 overtakes of LMGT3 cars by Hypercars. Each overtake lap was matched to a lap by the same driver and car, in the same stint-lap band and flag state, with no slower car within 5 s.

**Result:** we measured the time lost per overtake at 0.38 s, 95% CI [0.29, 0.47], from a bootstrap that resamples races.

**Conclusion:** each pass of a slower car costs a Hypercar 0.38 s, and the simulator charges that much per overtake.
