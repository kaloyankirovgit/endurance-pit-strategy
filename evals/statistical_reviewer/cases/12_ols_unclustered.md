# Track temperature and pace

**Data:** 61,940 clean Hypercar laps from 21 races.

**Method:** OLS of each lap's residual (lap time minus the car's race median) on track temperature at that lap.

**Result:** 0.012 s slower per °C, standard error 0.0009, p < 0.001.

**Conclusion:** track temperature has a clearly established effect on Hypercar pace.
